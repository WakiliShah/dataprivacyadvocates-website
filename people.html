The Founder.

A solo practice, deliberately — direct, personal, partner-led counsel with no layers between you and the advocate handling your matter.

Founding Partner

Patrick Muchangi

Patrick Muchangi is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and the founder of Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates. He built his practice in litigation — criminal defence and civil disputes, including two reported High Court murder acquittals and a land dispute appeal — before relocating to Nairobi in 2026 to specialise in data protection and AI governance.

He is currently completing formal certification in this specialism, building on a year of sustained independent study. His focus is helping SMEs, startups and organisations adopting AI tools translate legal obligations into practical compliance — bringing the same evidentiary rigour that won contested courtroom arguments to compliance and governance advisory. Every engagement is handled personally; there is no junior team it gets passed down to.

    Litigation Record

    Notable matters

    Republic v Muthoni

    [2023] KEHC 17976 (KLR). Lead defence counsel; full acquittal in a High Court murder trial at Nyahururu.

    Republic v Komba

    [2026] KEHC 4850 (KLR). Defence counsel; acquittal at close of the prosecution's case in a High Court murder trial.

    Chege v Ngumba

    [2024] KEELC 3499 (KLR). Appellate representation in an Environment and Land Court dispute.

    Law Society of Kenya v Attorney General & 3 others

    [2023] KEELC 20682 (KLR). Acted for LSK in a constitutional petition challenging GMO maize importation (unsuccessful).

    LSK v Chief Registrar of the Judiciary & 4 others

    [2025] KEHC 4690 (KLR). Acted for LSK protecting two minors' privacy; secured a favourable ruling restraining disclosure of their identities.

    Also represented several further clients pro bono in murder trials reported in the Kenya Law Reports.
