Founding Partner

Patrick Muchangi

Patrick Muchangi is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and the founder of Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates, a specialist practice focused on data protection, AI governance, technology law, and digital regulation. He advises technology companies, financial institutions, healthcare providers, public bodies, and high-growth businesses on designing resilient compliance and governance frameworks that anticipate regulatory risk before it materializes.

His practice is built around engineering defensive regulatory architecture — transforming legal obligations into practical governance systems that withstand regulatory scrutiny, support innovation, and strengthen institutional trust. Every engagement is led personally, providing clients with strategic, high-touch counsel grounded in legal precision, commercial judgment, and a forward-looking understanding of the evolving digital economy.

    Compliance Advisor

    Elizabeth Wanjiru

    Elizabeth Wanjiru advises clients on data privacy, data protection and regulatory compliance, with particular focus on data controller and data processor registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

    She works alongside our advocates to translate data protection law into practical compliance action — helping organisations determine their registration obligations under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, prepare and submit ODPC filings, and build the ongoing processes needed to stay compliant.
