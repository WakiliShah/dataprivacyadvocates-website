You can't govern what you can't see.

Today's organisations depend on information, technology and artificial intelligence more than ever before. That dependence brings a governance question every leadership team has to answer: do you have independent, evidence-based visibility over how these assets are managed — or are you relying on assurances from the same teams responsible for running them?

Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates exists to answer that question. We combine legal expertise, litigation-honed diligence and evidence-based assessment to help founders and executive teams understand, govern and continuously improve their Digital Trust posture — across data protection, cybersecurity, AI governance and cross-border compliance.
