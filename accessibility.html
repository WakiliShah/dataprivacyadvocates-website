Accessibility Statement.

Our approach to making this site usable for everyone, and how to report an accessibility issue. Last updated: July 2026.

Our commitment

We want this site to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using assistive technologies such as screen readers, or navigating primarily by keyboard. We have built the site with semantic HTML, descriptive image alt text, visible focus states, and a responsive layout that works across desktop and mobile devices.

What we have done

Descriptive alt text
All meaningful images across the site carry descriptive alt attributes for screen reader users.
Keyboard navigation
Navigation menus, forms, and buttons are operable via keyboard, not just mouse or touch.
Responsive design
Pages adapt to your screen size and support browser text resizing without breaking layout.
Readable structure
Content uses proper heading hierarchy and semantic markup to support navigation by screen reader.

Ongoing work & feedback

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time fix, and we are continuing to review and improve this site. If you encounter any barrier using this website - a page that does not work well with your screen reader, a form you cannot complete, or anything else - please tell us. We take these reports seriously and will work to fix genuine issues promptly.

Contact us at consult@dataprivacyadvocates.co.ke or WhatsApp 0736 358 938.

Notice something we should fix?

Accessibility improvements are ongoing - let us know if a page or form is not working well for you.
