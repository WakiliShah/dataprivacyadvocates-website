Cookie Notice.

This notice explains how Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can manage your preferences. Last updated: July 2026.

What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help a site remember your preferences, keep it functioning correctly, and, where you've agreed to it, help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

How we use cookies

We do not run advertising or advertising cookies. We do use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand, in aggregate, which pages and resources visitors find useful — but analytics cookies only activate after you choose "Accept All" in the cookie banner. If you choose "Essential Only," analytics stays switched off for your visit. The table below reflects what actually runs on this site today, not a generic template.
