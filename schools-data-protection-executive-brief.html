Executive Brief No. 004

Protecting the Child Behind the Form

Digital compliance for Kenyan schools — admissions portals, biometric attendance, learner photographs and third-party EdTech platforms, and the registration obligation most schools don't know applies to them.

Prepared by Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates

Executive Brief No. 004 July 2026 Reading Time: 9 Minutes Educational Publication

Executive Summary

Kenyan schools now run most of their operations through digital channels — online admissions, biometric attendance, school management platforms, and websites promoting school life through photographs of learners. Each of these channels collects, stores or displays data belonging to children, who are among the categories of data subject the Data Protection Act 2019 protects most closely.

Fewer schools realise that the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 name “educational institutions” as a sector subject to mandatory registration with the ODPC — regardless of turnover or employee count. The general small-entity exemption (turnover under KES 5 million and fewer than 10 employees) does not apply to schools. Most Kenyan schools that have never registered are not exempt; they are simply unregistered.

This Executive Brief sets out what that means in practice — and the questions every Board of Governors and proprietor should be able to answer about how their school governs the data of the children in its care.

Why This Matters

A registration duty most schools have never heard of.

A Registration Duty Most Schools Miss

Educational institutions must register with the ODPC regardless of turnover or headcount — the small-entity exemption does not apply.

Reputational Exposure

A single mishandled admissions form or leaked class photo reaches parents faster than any regulator ever could.

Executive Brief Overview

Six issues across a learner's digital record.

Each issue below is examined in full within the Executive Brief, from admission through to the data a school still holds long after a learner has left.

01

Registration with the ODPC

Educational institutions are named as a mandatory registration category under the Data Protection (Registration) Regulations, 2021 — irrespective of the school's size or turnover.

02

Online Admissions & Enrolment

Whether admission forms collect only what is necessary, and whether unsuccessful applicants' data is ever deleted.

03

Parental Consent for a Child's Data

Whether there is a distinct, recorded consent step for a child's data — separate from the general terms of enrolment.

04

Photographs & Videos of Learners

Whether publishing images on a website or social media rests on a documented consent or opt-out mechanism.

05

Biometric Systems & EdTech Vendors

Necessity and proportionality of biometric attendance data, and data processing agreements with School Management System providers.

06

CCTV & Physical Safeguarding

Signage, retention and a clear policy distinguishing safeguarding from surveillance, particularly around classrooms.

Questions Worth Asking at the Next Board Meeting

Seven questions to put to school leadership this term.

  • Has our school ever registered as a data controller with the ODPC?
  • Is there a distinct, recorded parental consent step for each category of a child's data we collect?
  • Do we have a signed data processing agreement with every EdTech and School Management System vendor?
  • Is there a documented, opt-out-capable process for publishing photographs of learners?
  • Was the necessity of any biometric data collection for learners formally assessed before deployment?
  • Does the Board of Governors receive any reporting on data protection compliance at all?
  • If the ODPC inspected our school tomorrow, could we produce a certificate of registration?
