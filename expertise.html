Five practice areas. One area of deep expertise.

We chose to specialise rather than generalise. Every practice group below sits inside the same discipline: technology, data and regulatory compliance.

01

Data Protection & Privacy

Comprehensive compliance counsel under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

  • Privacy notices, data processing agreements and terms of use
  • ODPC registration and Data Protection Officer appointment — now available as a fixed-fee package
  • Data Protection Impact Assessments (Section 31)
  • Data breach preparation, response and notification
  • Representation before the ODPC and on appeal to the High Court — see our case notes on recent ODPC determinations
See the fixed-fee ODPC registration package →
02

GDPR & Cross-Border Compliance

For businesses with EU touchpoints or cross-border data flows.

  • Assessing whether and how GDPR applies to your business
  • Cross-border transfer compliance (Section 48, DPA 2019)
  • Cookie consent banners and online privacy notices
  • Data processing agreements with international vendors
  • Data inventories and mapping
03

Fintech & Startup Advisory

From formation to fundraising, for founders and technology businesses.

  • Company incorporation under the Companies Act, 2015
  • SAFE notes, convertible notes and investor agreements
  • SaaS agreements, software licensing and terms of use
  • Regulatory guidance for fintech and digital lending products
04

Board & Governance Advisory

Helping boards build compliance into how the organisation runs.

  • Data governance frameworks and internal policy development
  • DPO structures and reporting lines
  • Compliance programme design and staff training
  • Vendor and third-party risk review
05

AI Governance & Emerging Tech

Regulatory readiness for AI and emerging technology deployment.

  • AI governance frameworks and risk assessments
  • Data usage and AI procurement agreements
  • Monitoring emerging AI-specific regulation in Kenya
  • Cybersecurity compliance under the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018
In Focus

Breach response & cybersecurity readiness.

Data protection isn't only about policy — it's about being ready when something goes wrong. We help clients prepare incident response plans, meet ODPC breach notification timelines, and review vendor and platform security.
