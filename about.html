The practice

“The goal is not simply to tell you what the law says. It is to help you decide what to do next.”

Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates is a Nairobi-based legal practice focused on data protection, privacy, technology law, cybersecurity and AI governance. The practice combines litigation discipline with practical advisory work: identifying the legal issue, testing the risk, explaining the options and helping the client act.

What working with us feels like

Start with the problem. We help you find the legal path.

You do not need to arrive with the right legal terminology. Tell us what happened, what you are building, what the regulator has asked for, or what decision is keeping you awake. We will help frame the legal issue.

01

Listen

We understand the facts, context and outcome you are trying to achieve.

02

Frame

We identify the legal, regulatory and commercial questions that actually matter.

03

Advise

We explain the position plainly, including options, risks and practical next steps.

04

Act

Where instructed, we help implement the advice, engage regulators or protect your position.
