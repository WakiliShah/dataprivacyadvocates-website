A litigator's discipline, applied to data protection and AI governance.

As organisations increasingly depend on data, technology and artificial intelligence, they need counsel who can test a compliance position the way a courtroom would test it. That rigour — built through years litigating in the High Court — is what Muchangi Patrick & Associates Advocates brings to data protection and AI governance advisory.

Our Story

Built on litigation, now focused on data and AI.

Patrick Muchangi & Associates Advocates was founded in 2023 as a general litigation practice in Laikipia County, handling criminal defence and civil disputes, including land matters. That practice produced a track record that is unusual for a young firm: reported High Court decisions, including two murder acquittals argued to full defence.

In 2026, following a relocation to Nairobi, the practice began a deliberate specialisation in data protection and AI governance — an area of sustained independent study, with formal certification currently underway. We bring the same evidentiary discipline that won those courtroom arguments to compliance and governance advisory: testing assumptions, not accepting them.

Notable Matters

A track record built in the courtroom.

Republic v Muthoni

Criminal Case No. 19 of 2018; [2023] KEHC 17976 (KLR). Lead defence counsel in a High Court murder trial at Nyahururu. Secured a full acquittal, showing the prosecution's case rested on unheard witnesses and hearsay, with no forensic link to the accused.

Republic v Komba

Criminal Case No. E024 of 2023; [2026] KEHC 4850 (KLR). Defence counsel in a High Court murder trial at Nyahururu. Achieved acquittal at the close of the prosecution's case, exposing gaps in phone and forensic evidence.

Chege v Ngumba

Environment and Land Appeal 49 of 2023; [2024] KEELC 3499 (KLR). Appellate representation in a land dispute before the Environment and Land Court.

Law Society of Kenya v Attorney General & 3 others

Environment and Planning Petition 2 of 2023; [2023] KEELC 20682 (KLR) (12 October 2023). Acted for the Law Society of Kenya in a constitutional petition challenging the government's approval of GMO maize importation. The petition was unsuccessful, but reflects experience in high-profile public interest litigation.

Law Society of Kenya v Chief Registrar of the Judiciary & 4 others

Constitutional Petition E003 of 2024; [2025] KEHC 4690 (KLR) (8 April 2025). Acted for LSK, suing as next friend of two minors, in a petition to protect their privacy and dignity. Secured a favourable ruling restraining the respondents from disclosing the minors' identities and ordering redaction of their names from all case records.

Patrick has also represented several further clients, on a pro bono basis, in murder trials reported in the Kenya Law Reports.
