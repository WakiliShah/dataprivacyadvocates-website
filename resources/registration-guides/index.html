Beyond Registration

Registration is the first step. We help with everything after it.

A certificate confirms you're on the register — it doesn't appoint your Data Protection Officer, write your privacy notice, vet your vendor contracts, or defend you if the ODPC opens a file. That's the ongoing work of a Data Protection & Privacy Law practice, and it's where most compliance risk actually sits.

Compliance RetainerDPO-as-a-service, DPIAs, privacy notices and vendor agreements, on an ongoing basis.
Registration & RenewalEnd-to-end ODPC registration, correction notices, and 24-month renewal tracking.
