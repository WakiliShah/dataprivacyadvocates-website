Why Financial Services

The digital risks unique to financial institutions.

Digital lending, mobile money and AI-driven credit scoring have made financial services one of the most active sources of ODPC and High Court data protection disputes in Kenya.

Credit & Identity Data

KRA PINs, national IDs and credit histories are processed at scale, and recycled phone numbers have repeatedly ended up in court.

AI Credit Scoring

Automated fraud detection and credit-scoring tools raise fairness, transparency and Board-oversight questions regulators are only beginning to ask.

Outsourced Core Systems

Core banking platforms, payment processors and credit reference bureaus all process member and customer data on the institution's behalf.

Enforcement & Appeals

SACCOs and lenders account for a significant share of ODPC awards, appeals and garnishee disputes reaching the High Court.
